= Seals and signatures
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A {term-seal} is an advanced electronic signature that a public body applies as an organization, not as a person.
It proves which body issued a record, and that nobody changed the record since.
These rules set the formats, the levels, the algorithms, the keys and the trust anchors of seals.
They apply to the issuer, and to any verifier that checks a seal.

The formats come from the list that public bodies of the European Union must accept from 23 February 2027.
The research for this specification found no Canadian or Québec list.
The European list costs an issuer nothing.
It also lets validators of public bodies abroad read the seals.

== Formats

[[LOG-010]]
*LOG-010.* An issuer MUST seal each record in a format of this table, at the version that it gives.
The table follows Annex I of link:https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2026/248/oj/eng[Implementing Regulation 2026/248 of the European Union].
An issuer MUST NOT use the older baseline formats of Annex II for a new seal.
That annex keeps them only for signatures made before 23 February 2028.

[cols="1,3,2",options="header"]
|===
|Format |Standard and version |Records
|JAdES |link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/119100_119199/11918201/01.02.01_60/ts_11918201v010201p.pdf[ETSI TS 119 182-1 V1.2.1] |{abbr-json-first}
|PAdES |link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31914201/01.02.01_60/en_31914201v010201p.pdf[ETSI EN 319 142-1 V1.2.1] |{abbr-pdf-first}
|XAdES |link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31913201/01.03.01_60/en_31913201v010301p.pdf[ETSI EN 319 132-1 V1.3.1] |{abbr-xml-first}
|CAdES in an ASiC-E container |link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31912201/01.03.01_60/en_31912201v010301p.pdf[ETSI EN 319 122-1 V1.3.1], in the container of link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31916201/01.01.01_60/en_31916201v010101p.pdf[ETSI EN 319 162-1 V1.1.1] and link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/319100_319199/31916202/01.01.01_60/en_31916202v010101p.pdf[ETSI EN 319 162-2 V1.1.1] |Any other file, and bundles of files
|===

[[LOG-011]]
*LOG-011.* An issuer MUST choose the format from the media type of the record, under link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc6838[RFC 6838].
A {abbr-json} record takes JAdES, a {abbr-pdf} record takes PAdES, and an {abbr-xml} record takes XAdES.
Any other record, and any record made of several files, takes a detached CAdES seal inside an ASiC-E container.
The container MAY also hold time stamps that the issuer adds before logging.
A file added after logging would change the logged bytes of LOG-031.
Each format then signs the bytes that its readers already know how to open.

[[LOG-012]]
*LOG-012.* A JAdES seal MUST carry the `x5c` header parameter of link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc7515[RFC 7515], with the certificate chain of the seal.
The parameter MAY sit in the signed or the unsigned header.
Annex I of Regulation 2026/248 adds this demand to JAdES.
A validator then finds the certificate in the seal itself, without a call to the issuer.

[[LOG-013]]
*LOG-013.* An issuer MUST seal each record at level B-LT or higher.
Levels B-B and B-T alone do not conform.
At level B-LT, the seal holds the certificates and the revocation data that a validator needs.
The seal then stays verifiable after its certificate expires, or after its certificate authority closes.
An issuer MAY seal at level B-LTA.

An augmentation to B-LTA after logging makes a new file, and a verifier still needs the logged original of LOG-031.
For long retention, the evidence records of LOG-070 protect many records at once.

The federal guidance on electronic signatures also asks for long-term validation data in these formats.

== Signers and algorithms

[[LOG-014]]
*LOG-014.* An issuer SHOULD seal its records with an organizational seal, whose certificate names the public body.
It SHOULD use the signature of a named official only where a law names a person who must sign.
An organizational seal does not depend on one person, so it survives a change of staff.
The link:https://www.govinfo.gov/about/authentication[publishing office of the United States] seals its official records this way.
Whether a seal counts as a signature under the law of a {term-jurisdiction} is a question for counsel.

[[LOG-015]]
*LOG-015.* A seal MUST use one of these algorithms.

* ECDSA on the curve P-384 of link:https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/186-5/final[FIPS 186-5], with SHA-384.
An issuer SHOULD choose this one.
* RSA with a modulus of 3072 bits or more, with SHA-384 or a stronger hash.

A seal MUST NOT use any other algorithm.
In particular, this specification keeps Ed25519 for the keys of the log.
Both choices appear in link:https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/cryptographic-algorithms-unclassified-protected-protected-b-information-itsp40111[ITSP.40.111], the federal guidance on algorithms.
ETSI TS 119 312 V2.1.1 ends RSA keys under 3000 bits on 31 December 2026.
P-384 is also the classical half of the hybrid that ETSI recommends for long-term seals.
A later change then adds a key and keeps this one.

[[LOG-016]]
*LOG-016.* An issuer MUST keep each seal key in a hardware security module that keeps the key inside it.
The module MUST hold a certification to FIPS 140-3 level 3, or to Common Criteria EAL4 or higher.
The key MUST follow SEC-020 and SEC-023, including its rotation and its owner.
The rules on qualified preservation services of the European Union ask for the same level of hardware.
A seal key signs every record of a body.
Its theft would let a forger issue records in the name of the state.

== Trust anchors

[[LOG-017]]
*LOG-017.* An issuer MUST publish each {term-trust-anchor} set that verifiers need for its seals and its time stamps.
Each set MUST have a name, a stable {abbr-https-first} address and a period of validity.
It MUST list the root certificates, or point to a trusted list that holds them.
Here are some examples.

* The certificate authorities that the President of the Treasury Board recognizes under SOR/2005-30.
* The roots of the public key infrastructure of the Québec government.
* The list of trusted lists of the European Union.

A verifier MUST NOT trust a seal through an anchor that no published set of the issuer names.
The research found no trusted list of the European kind in Canada or Québec.
Each issuer must therefore say which anchors apply.

[[LOG-018]]
*LOG-018.* Each seal MUST record the trust-anchor set that applies to it, and the assurance level of its certificate.
The level MUST name a scheme and a value from this table.

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Scheme |Values |Source
|`tbs` |`1`, `2`, `3`, `4` |The four assurance levels of the {abbr-tbs-first} guidance on electronic signatures
|`icpg` |`base`, `moyen`, `eleve` |The three levels of signature certificates of the Québec government public key infrastructure
|`eidas` |`advanced`, `qualified` |The kinds of seals of the {abbr-eidas-first}
|===

A verifier can then show which anchor and which level stood behind each seal.
A record does not then depend on one trust list that may change.

[[LOG-019]]
*LOG-019.* An issuer MUST keep a seal manifest for each sealed record, with the record.
The manifest is a {abbr-json} object with the fields of this table, named under NAM-040 to NAM-043.
It MUST NOT hold the salt of LOG-031, the content of the record or any personal information.
An issuer MAY publish the manifest.
The file `examples/seal-manifest.json` shows a manifest.

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Field |Content
|`id` |An opaque identifier of the record, a {abbr-uuid-first} version 7
|`profile` |`seal`, `seal-log` or `seal-log-preserve`, under LOG-002
|`mediaType` |The media type of the record before sealing
|`format` |`jades`, `pades`, `xades` or `asic-e`, under LOG-010
|`formatVersion` |The standard and version of the format
|`level` |`b-lt` or `b-lta`, under LOG-013
|`signerType` |`organizational-seal` or `personal-signature`, under LOG-014
|`algorithm` |The seal algorithm, such as `ecdsa-p384-sha384`, under LOG-015
|`certificateSha256` |The SHA-256 hash of the seal certificate, in hexadecimal
|`trustAnchorSet` |The address of the trust-anchor set, under LOG-017
|`assuranceLevel` |An object with `scheme` and `value`, under LOG-018
|`sealedAt` |The instant of the seal, in {abbr-utc-first}
|`timeStampAuthority` |The address of the time-stamp authority, under LOG-020
|`retentionRule` |The rule of the retention schedule, under OBS-102
|`log` |For the `seal-log` profiles: `origin`, `index` and `entryHash` of the log entry, under LOG-031
|`replaces` |Optional: the `id` of the record that this one replaces, under LOG-082 and LOG-083
|`hybridWith` |Optional: for a seal of a hybrid pair, an object with the `algorithm` and the `certificateSha256` of the other seal, under LOG-091
|===
