= Long-term preservation
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

Some records must stay verifiable for decades, such as laws, the titles of land and the records of civil status.
Their seals will not last that long.
Certificates expire, certificate authorities close, and algorithms weaken.
Canada, the European Union and the United States plan to retire today's signature algorithms between 2030 and 2035.

An {term-evidence-record} answers that problem with hashes and time stamps.
It time-stamps the root of a Merkle tree over many records.
Before an algorithm weakens, a preservation service adds a new time stamp or hash tree over the old proof.
The record then keeps a chain of proofs that it existed unchanged at each step.
One renewal covers millions of records.

The seal proves origin at issuance, and the log proves publication.
The evidence record proves that the record stays intact over the long term.
No single algorithm failure breaks all three.
These rules apply to the issuer and to the preservation service.

== Evidence records

[[LOG-070]]
*LOG-070.* A preservation service MUST keep an evidence record for each record of the `seal-log-preserve` profile.
The evidence record MUST follow link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc4998[RFC 4998] or link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc6283[RFC 6283].
It MUST cover the logged bytes of LOG-031, the proof of LOG-050 and the validation data of the seal.
The service MUST keep each evidence record beside the logged original, and not embed it in the record.
An embedded evidence record would make a new file, which the leaf of the log does not match.
The service MUST add each record to an evidence record before the time-stamp certificate of its seal expires.

An evidence record does not depend on the seal key.
It still protects the record after that key becomes unsafe.

[[LOG-071]]
*LOG-071.* The hash trees of each evidence record MUST use SHA-384, SHA-512, SHA3-384 or SHA3-512.
The log keeps SHA-256 under OBS-111, and this rule does not change that.
ETSI TS 119 312 V2.1.1 warns that SHA-256 may not suffice for archives kept beyond 20 years.
The long-term strength therefore sits in the evidence record, not in the log.

[[LOG-072]]
*LOG-072.* A preservation service MUST renew each evidence record in these cases.

* It adds a new time stamp before the algorithm, key size or certificate of the latest one stops being safe.
* Before its hash algorithm stops being safe, it builds a new hash tree over the record and the old proof.
* At each change of the cryptographic suite of the deployment, under LOG-092.

The service MUST review its algorithms at least once a year, against ETSI TS 119 312 and ITSP.40.111.
Each renewal MUST take its time stamp from an authority of LOG-020.
Once an authority offers it, renewals SHOULD use a time stamp that SLH-DSA or a hybrid pair signs.
SLH-DSA is the algorithm of link:https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/205/final[FIPS 205].
ETSI TS 119 312 recommends a hybrid with SLH-DSA for time stamps.
After such a renewal, a quantum computer can no longer forge the proof that the record existed before.

[[LOG-073]]
*LOG-073.* An issuer MUST state, for each series of the `seal-log-preserve` profile, a minimum verification lifetime.
That lifetime MUST equal the retention period of the series.
It MUST have no end for records that the issuer keeps permanently.
The preservation service MUST keep, for that lifetime, all data that a verifier needs.
That data includes the certificates and the revocation data of the seals and the time stamps.
It also includes the trust-anchor sets, the log keys and each version of the witness policy.

A proof that nobody can check after the data is gone proves nothing.

[[LOG-074]]
*LOG-074.* To preserve signatures, a preservation service SHOULD follow the model of link:https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_ts/119500_119599/119511/01.01.01_60/ts_119511v010101p.pdf[ETSI TS 119 511 V1.1.1].
An archive that holds the sealed records SHOULD pack them as link:https://www.iso.org/standard/87471.html[ISO 14721:2025] describes.

The European Union applies these two references to its qualified preservation and archiving services.
In Germany, the federal office for the security of information describes evidence records for public archives in its guideline link:https://www.bsi.bund.de/EN/Themen/Unternehmen-und-Organisationen/Standards-und-Zertifizierung/Technische-Richtlinien/TR-nach-Thema-sortiert/tr03125/BSITR03125.html[TR-03125].

[[LOG-075]]
*LOG-075.* A preservation service MUST keep an evidence record of each shard with entries of the `seal-log-preserve` profile.
It MUST cover the final checkpoint, its cosignatures and its time stamp of LOG-023.
It MUST also cover each version of the witness policy.
The proof of publication then outlives the Ed25519 keys of the log and of the witnesses.
