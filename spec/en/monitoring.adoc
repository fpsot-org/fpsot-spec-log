= Monitoring and incident response
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A seal proves that a key signed a record.
It cannot prove that the issuer meant to issue that record.
In 2021, people with valid access issued forged health certificates in the European Union, link:https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2021/12/07/eu-key-management/[without any stolen key].
Signature checks cannot catch that kind of failure.
An append-only log, read by a {term-monitor} that knows what the issuer meant to issue, can catch it.

These rules set how the issuer watches its own log and how it splits duties.
They also set how it responds when a key or an algorithm fails.
They apply to the issuer, to the log operator and to each monitor.

== Register and monitor

[[LOG-060]]
*LOG-060.* An issuer MUST keep a register of each record that it seals.
Each entry MUST give the `id` of the record and its seal manifest.
It MUST also give the role that asked for the seal and the role that approved it.
For the `seal-log` profiles, it MUST also give the shard and the index of the log entry.
The register is the list of what the issuer meant to issue, against which a monitor compares the log.

[[LOG-061]]
*LOG-061.* An issuer MUST run a monitor, or have another party run one for it.
The monitor MUST read each new entry and each new checkpoint of each shard, through the interface of LOG-036.
It MUST raise an alert in each of these cases.

* An entry matches no record of the register.
* A checkpoint does not extend the last one that the monitor saw.
* A key that the policy does not name signed a checkpoint.
* A certificate that the issuer did not list sealed a record of the register.
* A checkpoint lacks its quorum for longer than the delay of LOG-046.

Each alert MUST reach the roles that the issuer names, and MUST create an audit event under LOG-064.
The monitor MUST run under a role other than the sealing service.
Anyone else MAY run a monitor, since the log is public.

== Separation of duties

[[LOG-062]]
*LOG-062.* The role that may ask for a seal MUST differ from the role that administers the seal key.
Each request for a seal MUST come from a person or a service that the identity and access specification authenticates.
A second person MUST approve a request above a volume that the issuer sets, or outside the usual record types.
Generating, backing up or rotating a seal key MUST need two people.
One insider then cannot issue records alone, and the register shows who asked and who approved.

== Incidents

[[LOG-063]]
*LOG-063.* An issuer MUST keep a written plan for these incidents, and MUST test it at least once a year.

* A seal key or a log key leaks.
* A flaw appears in the way someone generated a key.
* An algorithm of a seal, a time stamp or the log becomes unsafe.

The plan MUST cover these steps.

1. Revoke the certificates and keys at stake, and close the shard at stake under LOG-035.
2. Find every record at stake, through the register and the log.
3. Seal those records again, with a transfer record under LOG-083.
4. Tell the people and bodies that rely on the records, and publish a notice.

In 2017, a link:https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2017-15361[flaw in a chip] forced Estonia to suspend the certificates of about 760,000 identity cards.
A plan that someone tested before the incident makes the response a routine.

== Events

[[LOG-064]]
*LOG-064.* An issuer and a log operator MUST record these events as audit events of the observability specification.
Each event MUST have the content of OBS-027.
The names follow NAM-050 and NAM-051.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Event |When
|`org.fpsot.log.record.sealed` |The issuer seals a record.
|`org.fpsot.log.record.resealed` |The issuer seals a record again, under LOG-083.
|`org.fpsot.log.record.withdrawn` |The issuer withdraws a record, under LOG-082.
|`org.fpsot.log.checkpoint.signed` |The log operator signs a checkpoint.
|`org.fpsot.log.shard.closed` |The log operator closes a shard, under LOG-035.
|`org.fpsot.log.policy.published` |The issuer publishes a policy or a new version of it, under LOG-045.
|`org.fpsot.log.key.rotated` |The issuer or the log operator replaces a seal key or a log key.
|`org.fpsot.log.alert.raised` |The monitor raises an alert, under LOG-061.
|===

An event MUST NOT hold the content of a record or its salt.
It MUST NOT hold personal information about the people that the record names.
It identifies the record by its `id` only.
