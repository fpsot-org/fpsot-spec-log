# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT signed records and transparency log specification.

The specification covers how a public body seals the records that it issues and keeps them verifiable: the formats, levels, algorithms and keys of electronic seals, trust-anchor sets and assurance levels, trusted time stamps, a public transparency log of salted hashes of sealed records with its checkpoints, shards and mirrors, witnesses and the published policy that verifiers apply, the offline proofs that go with each record and how verifiers check them, printouts with look-up codes, monitoring of the log and incident response, evidence records for long-term preservation, retention, redaction and transfer of sealed records and of log shards, and algorithm agility with a path to post-quantum seals. It includes example files and the scripts that check them.

It does not cover the logs, traces and metrics of software, or the tamper-evident audit log of a log store, which the FPSOT observability specification sets; the content of the records; the choice of a certificate authority, a time-stamp authority or a witness; or legal advice.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
